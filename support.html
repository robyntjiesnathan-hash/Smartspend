Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log an expense?

Tap the + button in the bottom navigation bar. Enter the amount, category, and a note, then tap Add.

How do I set a budget?

Go to the Budgets tab and tap + Add budget. Choose a category and set your monthly limit.

Can I use SmartSpend without an account?

Yes — tap Continue as guest on the sign-up screen. All your data will be saved locally on your device. You can create an account later to sync to the cloud.

How do I delete a transaction?

Go to the Transactions screen, find the transaction, and tap ✕ Delete. Confirm by tapping Delete again.

How do I sign out?

Go to Profile → Settings and scroll down to find the Sign out button.

How do I delete my account?

Email us at smartspendapp1@gmail.com and we'll delete your account and all associated data within 48 hours.
