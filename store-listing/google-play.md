## Full Description (4000 chars max)

Take control of your money with SmartSpend — the expense tracker that makes budgeting feel rewarding, not stressful.

SmartSpend helps you log every purchase in seconds, set spending limits that actually hold, and watch your savings goals grow — all wrapped in a streak system that keeps you coming back every day.

TRACK EVERY EXPENSE INSTANTLY
Log income and spending in a tap. Add an amount, pick a category, and you're done. See exactly where your money goes with a clear breakdown across Food, Transport, Shopping, Health, Entertainment, and more.

SET BUDGETS THAT WORK
Create monthly budgets for any category and get a clear progress bar showing how close you are to your limit. No surprises at the end of the month.

BUILD SAVINGS GOALS
Add savings goals with a target amount and deadline. Watch the progress bar fill as you save — whether it's for a holiday, an emergency fund, or your next big purchase.

EARN XP AND KEEP YOUR STREAK
Every expense you log earns XP. Log every day to keep your streak alive. SmartSpend turns good money habits into a daily routine you'll actually stick to.

WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT
Start tracking right away — no sign-up required. All your data stays privately on your device. Create an account whenever you're ready to sync across devices.

CLOUD SYNC ACROSS DEVICES
Sign up with your email to securely back up your data and access SmartSpend from any device. Your data is encrypted in transit and protected by database-level security.

CLEAN AND DISTRACTION-FREE
No ads. No subscriptions. No third-party trackers. Just a clean, fast app focused entirely on helping you spend smarter.

Free to download and use. No hidden fees.

## Content Rating
The app is suitable for all ages. It collects financial data entered by the user and an optional email address for account creation. No advertising. No in-app purchases.

## Data Safety Section Answers

**Data collected:**
- Email address (optional, for account creation only)
- Financial data (transactions, budgets, savings goals) — only if signed in

**Data NOT collected:**
- Location
- Device identifiers
- Usage data / analytics
- Advertising data

**Data shared with third parties:** No

**Data encrypted in transit:** Yes

**User can request deletion:** Yes (email privacy@smartspend.app)

## Google Play Requirements Checklist

- [ ] Feature graphic: 1024 x 500 px (required for store listing)
- [ ] Screenshots: min 2, recommended 4-8 (phone screenshots, 16:9 or 9:16)
- [ ] High-res icon: 512 x 512 px (use assets/icon.png — resize from 1024x1024)
- [ ] Privacy policy URL set in Play Console: https://robyntjiesnathan-hash.github.io/Smartspend/privacy.html
- [ ] Target API level: Android 14 (API 34) or higher — check compileSdkVersion
- [ ] AAB build: `eas build --platform android --profile production`
- [ ] Service account key: google-play-key.json (generate in Play Console > Setup > API access)
- [ ] Submit: `eas submit --platform android --profile production`
