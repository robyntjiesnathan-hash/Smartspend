## Description (4000 chars max)

Take control of your money with SmartSpend — the expense tracker that makes budgeting feel rewarding, not stressful.

SmartSpend helps you log every purchase in seconds, set spending limits that actually hold, and watch your savings goals grow — all wrapped in a streak system that keeps you coming back every day.

**TRACK EVERY EXPENSE INSTANTLY**
Log income and spending in a tap. Add an amount, pick a category, and you're done. See exactly where your money goes with a clear breakdown across Food, Transport, Shopping, Health, Entertainment, and more.

**SET BUDGETS THAT WORK**
Create monthly budgets for any category and get a clear progress bar showing how close you are to your limit. No surprises at the end of the month.

**BUILD SAVINGS GOALS**
Add savings goals with a target amount and deadline. Watch the progress bar fill as you save — whether it's for a holiday, an emergency fund, or your next big purchase.

**EARN XP AND KEEP YOUR STREAK**
Every expense you log earns XP. Log every day to keep your streak alive. SmartSpend turns good money habits into a daily routine you'll actually stick to.

**WORKS WITHOUT AN ACCOUNT**
Start tracking right away — no sign-up required. All your data stays privately on your device. Create an account whenever you're ready to sync across devices.

**CLOUD SYNC ACROSS DEVICES**
Sign up with your email to securely back up your data and access SmartSpend from any device. Your data is encrypted in transit and protected by row-level security.

**CLEAN, FAST, DISTRACTION-FREE**
No ads. No subscriptions. No third-party trackers. Just a clean, fast app that helps you spend smarter every day.
